# Pre-work pack — Cloud Sovereignty Self-Assessment (one page)

Six questions lean on records the workshop room will not hold (audit F-9 /
R-8). Each owner fills their card BEFORE the session; ten lines each is
enough. Bring the cards, not the source documents.

## Card 1 — Contracts desk (LEG / PROC)
For EACH critical provider:
- Governing law + venue of the main contract; any EU assets a judgment
  could reach. *(feeds SOV-2.governing-law)*
- Continuity / escrow / assignment-consent clauses: present? tested?
  *(feeds SOV-1.contract-survival)*
- Licences or subscriptions under a foreign export-control regime (US
  EAR/OFAC or equivalent): list them. *(feeds SOV-2.sanctions-exposure)*
- Audit rights: direct only, or tiered to sub-suppliers? Ever exercised?
  *(feeds SOV-5.supply-audit, SOV-7.audit-rights)*

## Card 2 — Facilities / estates management (FAC)
- Who operates each hosting site (building, power, cooling): operator name,
  contract jurisdiction, ownership chain. *(feeds SOV-3.facility-operator)*
- Energy: tracked mix? renewable share? EU guarantees of origin held?
  *(feeds SOV-8.energy)*
- Environmental reporting currently produced, and for whom.
  *(feeds SOV-8.stewardship)*

## Card 3 — Platform inventory (OPS)
- External chokepoints per dimension: licence/activation servers, update
  channels, CDN/DNS — with known grace periods. *(feeds SOV-4.kill-switch)*
- Hardware provenance extract for the highest-risk systems: vendor, known
  component origins, jurisdictions. *(feeds SOV-5.provenance)*

Everything else is answerable in the room. If a card is missing on the day,
answer *Don't know* — it is counted honestly and reported with the floor.
